The Quiet Place Initiative · A contemplative Christian community

Make room for stillness.
Find space for grace.

Inspired by the Christian tradition, we make room to pause, pray, and reconnect with God and one another. Come as you are—with faith, questions, or simply a need for quiet.

We are an organization founded on Protestant Christian beliefs. We may draw on the Bible from time to time, but nothing is imposed on anyone. All are welcome, wherever they are on their own path.

01 · What we value

Grace for the journey

We draw on the hope of the Gospel and the wisdom of Scripture, while making room for honest questions, different experiences, and each person's own path of faith.

02 · How we gather

Prayer, song, and stillness

Our gatherings bring together simple prayer, Scripture, music, and unhurried conversation. Take part in a way that feels comfortable; quiet presence is welcome, too.

03 · Why we are here

Care beyond ourselves

Following the example of Christ, we seek to listen with care, build genuine connections, and carry compassion into the everyday life of our neighbors.
